About

I'm a Doctoral Candidate in Management (Design & Innovation) at the Weatherhead School of Management, Case Western Reserve University, expecting to graduate in May 2027. My dissertation, Knowing, Trusting & Working in Metahuman Systems: A Longitudinal Ethnography of Everyday Knowledge Work, examines how professionals and AI systems develop epistemic trust and distribute cognition in practice. I defended the proposal in May 2025.

My research sits at the intersection of human–AI collaboration, sociotechnical systems, and critical algorithmic governance. I study how knowledge workers navigate trust, doubt, and delegation when AI enters their workflows — drawing on cognitive ethnography and distributed cognition frameworks.

Beyond academia, I'm a nonprofit co-founder with over a decade of experience building initiatives in diversity, inclusion, sustainability, and experiential education across India and the United States.
